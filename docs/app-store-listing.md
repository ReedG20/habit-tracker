## Version 1.0.0

**Promotional text (170):**

> Write a contract with yourself, prove it each day with a photo, a check-in or
> a timer, and choose what's on the line if you don't. Ante holds you to it.

**Description:**

> Ante turns a habit or a goal into a contract you sign. You decide what you'll
> do, how you'll prove it, and what happens if you don't follow through.
>
> MAKE IT SPECIFIC
> Name the habit or the goal and how often or by when. Ante helps you word it so
> one photo, one check-in or one timer can clearly show you did it.
>
> PROVE IT
> • Photo: snap it, and AI checks it against what you promised.
> • Location: check in from the gym, the library or the office.
> • Timer: keep Ante open while you read, practice or focus.
>
> PUT SOMETHING ON THE LINE
> Pick one consequence for each commitment:
> • Money: you choose an amount ($1–$50). Your card is charged once, only if
> you miss. Nothing is charged when you set it up.
> • Tell a friend: someone you pick gets an email if you miss.
> • Lockout: your habits freeze for a few days.
> • Just your word.
>
> Change your mind right after making one? Call it off. Think a charge was
> wrong? Contest it from the app.
>
> SEE IT THROUGH
> Streaks, milestones and a signed contract to look back on when you keep it.
>
> ANTE PRO
> Ante Pro unlocks the app: up to 10 habits and 6 goals at a time, with every
> kind of proof and stake. Monthly or yearly; the yearly plan can start with a free
> week. Payment is charged to your Apple ID when you confirm. It renews
> automatically unless cancelled at least 24 hours before the period ends, and
> you can manage or cancel it in Settings → Apple ID → Subscriptions.
>
> Money stakes are for people 18 and older.
>
